Questions

Frequently asked

What exactly is the guide?

A 63-page PDF with twenty-two steps. Each step is a teaching page and a full-page prompt you paste into Claude Code, plus a proof: a cold restart, a live commit, a file appearing on GitHub. You end with a personal AI operating system on your own machine: a memory of your business, a chief of staff that returns decisions, your documents and tools connected, and guards on anything irreversible.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The guide assumes a business owner with no coding background. The setup chapter walks through installing the tool, and every technical step is a prompt the tool runs for you while you say yes or no to what it shows.

What do I need to run it?

A paid Claude Code plan, a free GitHub account, and a computer that stays awake. About one afternoon for Part One, an evening for Part Two, and Part Three spread across a week.

How do I get the file?

Checkout runs through Stripe. Your download opens on the next page, and your receipt email carries the link. Updates to the guide are free: the download link always serves the current version.

Is this a subscription?

No. One payment, one file, yours to keep. There is nothing that stops working if you never talk to me again.

What is the difference between the guide and building it together?

The guide is self-guided and covers everything that runs as plain files. Building it together adds me on your machine for the parts a guide cannot ship: scheduled jobs that run unattended, gates that check outbound before it fires, and a live dashboard. It starts with a short call and is priced per engagement, from $5,000.

Can I return it?

No. It is a digital download, so all sales are final. If a step fails for you, write to andrew@andrewzoll.com with the step and the error and I will help you through it, and the guide gets fixed for everyone in the next version.
